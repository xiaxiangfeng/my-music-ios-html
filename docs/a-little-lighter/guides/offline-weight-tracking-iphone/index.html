How to Track Your Weight Offline on iPhone

A Little Lighter is a weight tracker and mood journal for iPhone. Once installed, it lets you record entries and view your weight history offline without creating an account. It works on iOS 17 or later and stores entries on your iPhone.

Record your weight and mood

  1. Open the app and tap Record.
  2. Select the date and enter your weight. Use the ruler or the plus and minus buttons to adjust it in 0.1 kg increments. The app records weight in kilograms.
  3. Choose a mood, add a note if you like, and save. Saving again for the same date updates that day’s entry.

Review your weight trends

Open Journey to see your history over 7, 30 or 90 days, or view all entries. Browse entries by month and tap one to edit it. You can view your history and update records with your iPhone offline.

Export your weight log as a CSV

  1. Open Journey and use its CSV export option to create a file, or use its copy option to paste the CSV into another app.
  2. Save a copy in a location you control, such as a local folder in Files. You can open the CSV in a spreadsheet app to sort or review your entries.

The CSV includes dates, weight values, moods and notes. Save a copy before deleting the app or switching phones. The current version can export your records for use elsewhere, but it can’t import the CSV to restore your journal.
