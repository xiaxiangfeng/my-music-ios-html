A few things to know.

What devices does A Little Lighter support?

The app runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. You can browse this website on a phone, tablet or computer.

Can I use it as an offline weight tracker?

Yes. You can record weight and mood, review your history and make cards offline. Your journal is stored on your iPhone.

What shows up on a shared card?

The front shows your cumulative change, a relative trend and any message you add. Your starting weight and current weight stay in your journal.

Which units and languages can I use?

Weight is recorded in kilograms (kg). Use the globe on the app’s Home screen to switch between English and Simplified Chinese.
