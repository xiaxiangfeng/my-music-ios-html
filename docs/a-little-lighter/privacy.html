A LITTLE LIGHTER / BODY DIARY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how A Little Lighter and its developer handle information when you use the iPhone app or these support pages.

At a glance

A Little Lighter is a personal weight and mood journal. No app account is required. We do not collect or receive your journal entries through the app, operate a journal server, or sell your personal information.

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. It does not connect to Apple Health.

Information on your iPhone

Dates, weight values, moods, notes, your plan name, starting weight and optional goal are stored in the app on your iPhone. The app uses these entries to calculate changes, draw charts and make cards on the device.

Your selected language, card style and personal card message are also saved locally. Journal records are not automatically uploaded or synchronized with another device.

Saving a card to Photos

The app requests add-only Photos permission when you choose to save a generated card. It does not request access to read your photo library. You can manage this permission in iOS Settings.

If you decline Photos permission, you can still record entries, view your journal, create cards and use the system share sheet. The app does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Sharing and exports you choose

When you use Share, iOS sends the generated image to the app or destination you select. Share cards show cumulative change and a relative chart; starting and current weight values stay off the card front. Your own card message may contain information you choose to write.

CSV exports contain dates, absolute weight values, moods and notes. Copying a CSV places that information on the system clipboard. Exporting a file or saving a card creates a copy outside the journal. Review the content and destination before sharing.

The developer does not receive a copy through these features. A destination app or storage provider handles the copies you send under its own privacy practices. Temporary share images are removed after the share sheet closes; temporary CSV files are removed when the export screen closes.

Keeping, deleting and backing up entries

Entries remain in the app until you delete them or delete the app. You can edit or delete entries in Journey and change your plan in My little goal. To remove the app's local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App preserves app documents and data.

The journal database is marked to be excluded from automatic device backups. Preferences may still be included in backups managed by iOS. This version supports CSV export for keeping a copy or viewing records elsewhere; it does not yet import CSV files or restore a journal backup.

Deleting an entry or the app does not delete cards in Photos, exported files, clipboard contents or copies already shared to another service. Manage those copies at their destinations. We cannot access or delete the journal on your iPhone remotely.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by the email providers involved in delivery and storage.

Please avoid including private journal records or other sensitive information unless you choose to do so for a specific support request. You can use the contact address below to ask us to delete support correspondence.

These support pages

These static pages do not contain advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or cookies set by us. When viewed through GitHub Pages or Cloudflare, the hosting provider may process technical information about a visit, including an IP address, under its own privacy policy. Website hosting is separate from the app's on-device journal.
