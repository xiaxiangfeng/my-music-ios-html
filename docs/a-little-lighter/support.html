A LITTLE LIGHTER / BODY DIARY

A Little Lighter Support

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Keep a personal weight journal, notice your changes and make a card that feels like you. A Little Lighter works without an account and supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Record a little moment

Tap Record, enter your weight in kilograms, choose the date and mood, and add an optional note. The + and − buttons and the ruler adjust the value in 0.1 kg steps. Saving another entry for the same day updates that day's entry.

Create and share a card

After saving, choose a paper style and a personal message in the card editor. You can also use Customize on Home. Swipe the card horizontally to change its style; use See details to turn it over. Scrolling the page does not turn the card over.

Share opens the iOS share sheet. Available destinations depend on the apps installed on your iPhone. You finish any post in the destination app. Save to Photos asks only for permission to add the card.

Edit entries and set a little goal

Open Journey to view charts and entries grouped by month. Tap an entry to edit it, or use its menu to delete it. A brief Undo option appears after deletion. My little goal lets you name a plan, keep a starting point and optionally set a goal.

Switch between English and Chinese

Use the globe at the top of Home to choose English or 简体中文. The app starts in English the first time and remembers your selection for the next launch. Your own notes and card messages keep the words you entered.

Keep a copy of your records

Journey provides CSV copy and export. CSV contains dates, weight values, moods and notes, so store or share it with care. It is useful for keeping a copy or opening records elsewhere; this version does not yet import CSV or restore a journal backup.

The journal stays on this iPhone and is excluded from automatic device backups. Deleting the app can remove the local journal. Saving a copy before removing the app is recommended.

If saving to Photos does not work

Check the app's Photos permission in iOS Settings, then try saving the card again. You can use Share even if you do not allow Photos access. If a problem continues, email the device model, iOS version and the steps that led to it; you do not need to send your journal.

Contact support

Email us with a description of the issue. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version when available. Only include screenshots or personal records that you are comfortable sending.
