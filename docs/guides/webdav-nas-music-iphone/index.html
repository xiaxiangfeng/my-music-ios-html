How to Play Music from a WebDAV NAS on iPhone

VinylPocket can browse music folders on your NAS over HTTPS WebDAV and add tracks to your iPhone’s record collection. The first time you play a NAS track, VinylPocket downloads the whole file. Once it’s cached, you can play it again offline.

What you’ll need

  • VinylPocket on an iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
  • A WebDAV music folder your iPhone can reach, with an HTTPS address and a certificate trusted by iOS. Use the folder URL, such as https://nas.example.com/music/.
  • A NAS username and password with permission to read the folder if your server requires Basic authentication.

Connect your NAS and add music

  1. Open VinylPocket and choose VINYL MODE → Connect NAS · WebDAV.
  2. Give the source a name, enter the HTTPS WebDAV folder address, and fill in your credentials if required. Tap Connect.
  3. Open Record Collection → Sources and choose the NAS you just added. Browse to a music folder, then tap a track to add it or choose Add All to add the audio files in that folder.
  4. Go back to your collection and select a track. Keep the connection to your NAS active until the track starts playing and is marked Cached in the collection.

Listen offline

Before leaving your network, play each track you want to take with you and let its first download finish. Check the Cached label in your collection, then try playing a cached track again with your iPhone disconnected. Adding a track or tapping Add All saves it to your collection, so it still needs a first play to download.

If you use Sources → Clear Cloud Cache, those NAS tracks need to download again. MP3s you imported from Files have their own local copies.

If your NAS won’t connect

  • Address or certificate error: Check that you entered the HTTPS WebDAV folder URL. Use your WebDAV endpoint rather than the NAS sign-in page or an SMB share address.
  • Access denied (401 or 403): Check your username and password, and make sure that user can list and read the music folder.
  • Works at home but not away: If your NAS is available only on your home network, download the tracks there first. You can play those cached tracks anywhere.
