VinylPocket Support

VinylPocket is an interactive 3D turntable for your own music. No music is included.

Import your music

Open Vinyl Mode and choose Import Music Files, or use the plus button in Record Collection. Select audio files through the Files app. Files are copied into VinylPocket; the originals remain where they are.

Connect a NAS

Open Sources and choose Connect NAS. Enter your server's HTTPS WebDAV URL, using a certificate trusted by iOS, and any required username and password. After connecting, browse folders and add music to your collection. The current version supports HTTPS WebDAV; direct SMB connections are not supported.

Use an audio link

Choose Add HTTPS Audio and enter a direct audio file URL. A webpage or HLS playlist is not supported. Remote music downloads completely before first playback. Fully cached files can be played offline; clear the cache from Sources when you want to free storage.

Play and organize

Tap the record or tonearm to play or pause. Drag the tonearm or musical progress line to seek, swipe the record to change tracks, and drag the volume slider to adjust volume. Add tracks from Record Collection to the queue and use the queue's drag handles to reorder them.

Deleting from the queue keeps the record in your collection. Deleting from the collection removes the app's copy and cache without deleting the original local or remote music file.
